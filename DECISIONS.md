# LearningOS Decisions

## Decision 001 — Local files are the source of truth

Our long-term learning state, plans, research findings, and decisions should not live only inside ChatGPT or Claude conversations.

Chats are for discussion and exploration.

Local files are the durable source of truth.

---

## Decision 002 — The system is flexible, not strict

The LearningOS plan is not a fixed roadmap.

We will change it when:

- research suggests something better
- actual learning data shows a problem
- priorities change
- a tool or workflow creates unnecessary friction

The process is:

Observe → Analyze → Decide → Try → Measure → Adjust

---

## Decision 003 — Software Development is the main priority

Current priority order:

1. Software Development
2. Thai
3. English

Learning how to learn is not a separate major subject.

It is a supporting layer used to improve the main learning tracks.

Japanese and UX are lower priority / paused for now.

Career and job resources are used when needed.

---

## Decision 004 — Course completion does not equal mastery

Finishing a video, lesson, chapter, or course does not mean the skill is mastered.

Mastery must be based on demonstrated ability.

Evidence may include:

- explaining
- reading and tracing code
- building
- debugging
- adapting knowledge to a new problem
- retaining the skill after time has passed

---

## Decision 005 — Delayed independent performance is strong mastery evidence

If meaningful time has passed and I can still perform the skill:

- without hints
- without explanations
- without being shown the solution
- preferably on a different or real problem

then this is strong evidence that the skill has been retained.

Immediate success directly after studying is weaker evidence.

---

## Decision 006 — AI help must be visible in assessment

A correct result is not enough by itself.

The system should distinguish between:

- independent work
- using documentation only
- receiving a small hint
- receiving guided help
- seeing a substantial part of the solution

A task completed with heavy AI assistance should not be treated the same as an independent success.

---

## Decision 007 — Knowledge gaps must be detected from evidence

We should not rely only on self-ratings such as:

"I think I know React 7/10."

Knowledge gaps should be inferred from evidence such as:

- repeated mistakes
- inability to recall
- inability to apply
- debugging difficulty
- dependence on hints
- failure on changed problems
- forgetting after a delay
- blockers encountered in real projects

---

## Decision 008 — Do not overbuild the system early

We will not automate everything immediately.

First:

- design the process
- use it manually or semi-manually
- observe what works
- collect useful evidence

Only then should we automate repetitive parts.

The goal is learning, not spending all our time building a learning-management system.

## Decision 009 — Baseline Before Specialization

I already have learning goals and technologies I want to pursue.

However, the initial Software Development baseline will intentionally remain as framework-neutral as practical.

The purpose is to discover my actual current capability and hidden gaps before allowing my preferred learning path to influence the diagnosis.

After the baseline:

- strong foundations may be skipped or compressed
- fragile foundations may be strengthened
- hidden prerequisite gaps may be repaired
- untested areas may be prioritized only when relevant
- specialization will then be aligned with my real learning goals

The baseline is not intended to change my goals automatically.

It is intended to show the strongest path toward those goals based on evidence.
